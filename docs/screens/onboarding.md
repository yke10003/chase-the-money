# Onboarding

**Not implemented.** Product intent only.

**Decision.** Onboarding takes about 3–4 minutes and must leave the user with a useful Home, not an empty state.

**Decision.** Do not collect profile data because it might be useful someday.

**Decision.** Onboarding should produce opportunities, not just a profile.

**Decision.** Do not run the full Money Hunt here. A lightweight asset-category question is enough. The full hunt lives in [Discover](discover.md).

## Likely flow

The steps below are the intended sequence. Copy, presets, and which steps can be skipped are not finalized. Amounts are examples.

### 1. Goal

How much extra money do you want to create?

Presets: €250, €500, €1,000, €2,000, custom.

Timeframe: this week, this month, next 30 days, custom.

### 2. Accepted categories

Earn, Sell, Recover, Save. The user can decline a category.

**Assumption.** A declined category is not suggested and does not count toward the goal. Opportunities of that type are not created during onboarding.

### 3. Work and skills

Current or recent work, and skills the user could realistically monetize.

Rough proficiency, if collected: Comfortable, Experienced, Expert.

**Decision.** Do not require a CV.

### 4. Willingness

Examples of what the user is open to:

- freelance work
- sell things
- build a product
- online gigs
- local jobs
- teach or advise
- buy and resell
- negotiate or recover money
- reduce expenses

Also learn what they explicitly do not want.

### 5. Available time

Examples:

- a few minutes here and there
- 1–3 hours/week
- 4–8 hours/week
- 8–15 hours/week
- 15+ hours/week

### 6. Starting capital

Examples: €0, up to €50, up to €250, up to €1,000, more if worthwhile.

This informs recommendations such as buy-and-resell. It is not a budget to manage.

### 7. Existing opportunities

Ask what is already in motion, and turn the answers into opportunities where possible.

Examples:

- someone owes me money
- I'm waiting on an invoice
- I have a freelance lead
- I have things I could sell
- I may be due a refund
- I have recurring expenses to review

### 8. Lightweight asset scan

Broad categories the user would consider selling. Not the full Money Hunt.

### 9. Social

Optional, and late. Skipping it is a complete onboarding.

### 10. First plan

**Decision.** The last step generates a plan. "You're all set" is not an ending.

Example:

```text
Your €2,000 October plan

Already identified:
€250 expected
€600+ sellable assets
1 freelance opportunity
1 outstanding invoice

Best next moves:
Value the Versace jacket
Contact 5 previous colleagues
Review subscriptions

First weekly target:
complete 4 actions
earn 150 XP
identify €500 of additional opportunity
```

The weekly numbers are examples from [gamification](../product/gamification.md), not finalized targets. The €600 sellable figure in this example is loose potential (jacket plus outfit, silver unknown) and must be labeled as potential, not expected or realized.

## Founder path

A manual walkthrough of onboarding should be able to capture the [founder fixture](../product/founder-fixture.md) without free-text being the only input: freelance lead, Versace jacket, silver, Halloween outfit, friend repayment, outstanding invoice, possible tax refund, subscription review, and an explicit "not now" for unrelated liabilities.
